Wojito Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Overview

Wojito stores pet-care data on your device. This version does not create an account and does not operate a cloud backend for your care logs.

1. Data We Store on Device

Animal and enclosure profiles, optional photo references, care notes, expense amounts, optional water readings, monthly recap outputs, Sample interaction state, preference flags, and StoreKit transaction identifiers used for consumable entitlements.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

This version does not require an email, password, or social login. It does not upload care logs to our servers. Sample community content is scripted and stays on device.

3. Permissions

Camera — Used when you tap Take Pet Photo to capture a pet or enclosure image for a profile.

Photos — Used when you tap Choose Pet Photo to pick an existing image, or Save Review Image to export a finished monthly recap.

Microphone — Used when you tap Dictate to turn speech into care notes or Sample update text. Keyboard entry always works if you decline.

App Tracking Transparency — A system prompt may appear the first time you open Animals Studio. This version does not track you across apps or read the advertising identifier for advertising.

Notifications — A system prompt may appear after the tracking prompt on first Animals Studio visit. This version does not send push or local notifications.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Wojito keeps verified transaction identifiers on device so a consumable gift pack is not granted twice. Entitlements cannot be restored after you delete the app or use Clear All Data.

5. Retention and Deletion

Data remains until you delete individual items, use Clear All Data, or remove the app. Clearing data permanently removes profiles, media copies, Sample history, and unused gift entitlements on this device.

6. Children

Wojito is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the product changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store product page support link for Wojito.
