Kisyza Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kisyza (“Kisyza,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the App. Kisyza is a local character-reel creation app for cosplay and character creators. This version does not create an account and does not post to a live community.

1. Summary

Most information stays on your device. We do not run a Kisyza user account or cloud profile. Purchases are processed by Apple. Camera, Photos, and Microphone are used only when you choose related actions. App Tracking Transparency and notification prompts may appear, but this version does not track you for advertising and does not send notifications.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use Kisyza, the App may store locally: costume photos or clips you capture or import (sandbox copies); audio you record for live convention sound, voice-over, or voice input; role card fields such as title and tags; draft and generation status for your character reel; finished local reel files and export status; verified StoreKit transaction identifiers and a local generation ledger for gift packs and treasure chests; preferences such as onboarding completion, theme, and whether home permission prompts already appeared. Demo browsing is local and scripted; Demo items are not real user profiles.

3. Information We Do Not Collect for Accounts

Kisyza does not ask you to register an email, phone number, username, or password. There is no in-app social graph, messaging, or cloud sync in this version.

4. Purchases and Payment Data

In-app purchases are Consumable gift packs and treasure chests sold at fixed US dollar prices ($0.99 through $99.99 as listed in the App). Apple processes payment. We do not receive your full payment card number. On device we may store a verified transaction identifier so the same purchase is not granted twice. Store listings do not state coupon or credit quantities. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payment processing.

5. Permissions

Camera is used after you tap Capture. Photos is used after you tap Import or Save to Photos. Microphone is used after you tap Record or Voice input. App Tracking Transparency may appear the first time you open Character Studio; this version does not use tracking and does not read the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Notifications may appear after the tracking prompt; this version does not send local or remote notifications. Allow or deny does not change core features.

6. How Local Data Is Used

On-device data is used only to run the character-reel workflow: media handling, audio, editing, free preview, confirmed generation, export, Gift Shop entitlements, settings, and Clear All Local Data. Preview does not consume a generation entitlement.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. This local prototype does not upload Your Content to our servers for community publishing. Apple may receive purchase and App Store-related information under Apple’s terms. If you save a reel to Photos, that file becomes part of your device photo library under your control.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until you delete a project where applicable, remove the App, or use Clear All Local Data in Settings. Clear All Local Data permanently deletes projects, media copies, Demo interaction history on device, preferences that reset prompts, and unused gift packs or treasure chests. Purchases cannot be restored after App removal or data clear. Because there is no account, there is no separate “delete account” process.

9. Children

Kisyza is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not use the App if you are under that age. If you believe a child provided data in the App, clear local data and uninstall, and contact us through App Store Support.

10. International Use

Data stays on your device unless you choose system features (such as device backups you control) or Apple purchase flows. Your use may be subject to local privacy laws.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: use the Support page in the App Store listing for Kisyza.
